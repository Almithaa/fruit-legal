This Privacy Policy explains how Grand Vision Heavy Industry Limited ("we", "us") handles information when you play Fruit Slash Party (the "Game") inside the TikTok platform.

1. Data We Collect

The Game does not require registration and does not collect personal information on our own servers. Game progress (such as your score, coins, gems and unlocked items) is stored locally on your device using local storage. When the Game uses TikTok's silent login, we receive a login code from TikTok; we use it only to derive a local player identifier so your progress stays consistent. We do not transmit this code to our own servers. If you view ads, advertising data is processed by TikTok's ad platform under TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

2. Third-Party Services

The Game runs inside TikTok and uses TikTok-provided capabilities, including login, sidebar revisit, add-to-home-screen shortcuts and (where available) advertising. TikTok may collect device identifiers, approximate location and usage data to serve ads and operate its platform, governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your TikTok account credentials.

3. Children

The Game is a casual arcade game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

4. Data Removal

You may remove all locally stored game data at any time by deleting the mini game or clearing TikTok's mini-program cache. Because we do not keep your game data on our servers, there is no account record of yours to delete on our side.

5. Security

Your game data is stored locally on your device. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance.
