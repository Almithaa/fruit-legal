Privacy Policy Terms of Service

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play Voxel Breaker (the "Game") inside TikTok. This policy applies only to Voxel Breaker, which is operated independently of any other game.

1. Local Game Data

The Game is a single-player puzzle. Level progress, stars, coins, unlocks, settings (including the frame-rate preference), and mission reward flags are stored on your device only. We do not create a game account, do not link your progress to your TikTok identity, do not sync progress across devices, and do not operate a game leaderboard or collect player profile information for the Game. Clearing the mini-game's data or changing devices may remove local progress.

2. TikTok Platform Capabilities

The Game does not call TikTok login or authorization APIs, does not create an account, and does not request profile or identity information. It may use TikTok's home-screen shortcut and profile-sidebar revisit missions, and TikTok's rewarded video API. Mission rewards are issued only when TikTok confirms eligibility and the required return visit has occurred. TikTok processes platform-account, mission, and advertising information under TikTok's own privacy policies, which are separate from this policy.

3. Optional Rewarded Advertising

The Game includes optional, player-initiated TikTok rewarded videos. You may choose to watch one to unlock extra launch slots, revive a stuck run, or double the coins earned on the results screen. A reward is granted only after TikTok reports that the video was watched to completion; closing it early or a playback failure grants nothing and preserves your progress. The Game does not show forced or interstitial advertisements. TikTok may process device and advertising interaction data to deliver and measure the video, under TikTok's own policies.

4. Purchases and Other Features

The Game has no in-app purchases, no game shop, no cross-device sync, no social leaderboard, and no user profile. Frame-rate and other display settings are saved locally on your device.

5. Children

The Game is a casual puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

6. Data Removal and Retention

Local progress, coins, and settings can be removed by clearing the mini-game's data in your TikTok app. The Game does not transmit this local data to a game backend. Information that TikTok processes to operate its own platform missions and advertising is handled under TikTok's policies.

7. Security

The Game package contains no TikTok client secret, no OAuth code exchange, and no game backend. Local progress remains on your device. We do not sell personal information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do.
