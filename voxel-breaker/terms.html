1. How the Game Works

Voxel Breaker is a local single-player puzzle. You rotate a three-dimensional voxel model, choose color packs from a queue, place them into a limited number of launch slots, and clear exposed blocks of matching color. Outer blocks peel away to reveal inner layers. The Game contains 600 puzzles across 20 chapters.

Level progress, stars, coins, unlocks, settings (including the 30 / 60 frame-rate option), and mission reward flags are stored on your device only. They are not tied to an account and are not synced. Clearing the mini-game's data, changing devices, or reinstalling may remove this local progress.

Video advertisements in Voxel Breaker are always optional and started by you. You may choose to watch a TikTok rewarded video to unlock extra launch slots, revive a run when no clearable move remains, or double the coins earned on the results screen. A reward is granted only after TikTok reports that the video was watched to completion. If playback fails, is closed early, or is unavailable, no reward is granted and your current progress is preserved. The Game does not force advertisements on you and does not use interstitial ads.
